On 22 October 2026 the Sejm passed an act that sets an upper limit on the pay of doctors, nurses and other health professionals in facilities with a National Health Fund (NFZ) contract (a government bill, print No. 3157). **229 MPs voted for, 144 against and 67 abstained** ([vote No. 149](https://www.sejm.gov.pl/Sejm10.nsf/agent.xsp?symbol=glosowania&NrKadencji=10&NrPosiedzenia=67&NrGlosowania=149), in Polish).

## How the clubs voted

The act was supported by MPs of KO (Civic Coalition), Lewica (The Left), Centrum (Centre), Polska 2050 (Poland 2050) and 29 of the 32 PSL (Polish People’s Party) MPs. The PiS club (Law and Justice) voted against. MPs of Rozwój Plus (Development Plus), Konfederacja (Confederation), Konfederacja Korony Polskiej (Confederation of the Polish Crown), Razem (Together) and Demokracja Bezpośrednia (Direct Democracy) abstained. The full results, including the votes on amendments, are in the [analysis](/en/analysis/medical-pay-limits-act/#how-the-clubs-voted).

## What the act contains

The limits are the same as in the bill adopted by the Health Committee:

- an hourly rate of no more than **1/20 of the minimum wage** (PLN 240.30 in 2026);
- a monthly cap of **8 times** the minimum wage for a full-time post with one provider, 12 times with allowances, and no more than **16 times** in total (PLN 76,896);
- no more than **two full-time posts** in total and at least a half-time post in a hospital;
- contracts only with individuals, not with intermediary companies, and a ban on pay calculated as a percentage of the NFZ contract.

## What the Sejm changed in the final vote

The Sejm adopted a minority motion that the committee had earlier rejected: **a ban on providing NFZ-funded services** for a doctor who has knowingly certified something untrue — for example about a service provided, working time or in a sick note. The ban is imposed by the President of the NFZ for between one month and 8 years, and for life for a repeated breach. Doctors under a ban will be entered in a **public register** with their first name, surname and licence number. These provisions will enter into force 6 months after the act is published ([print No. 3193](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/prints/3193/3193.pdf), minority motions No. 1, 3–6, 8–9; PDF, in Polish).

Amendments from the second reading ([print No. 3193-A](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/prints/3193-A/3193-A.pdf), PDF, in Polish) deleted two solutions from the committee’s version: terminations without Labour Code protection during the transitional period, and terminating a contract without notice when a health professional does not agree to a contract change that brings pay within the limit. The half-time hospital requirement will not apply to geriatricians, pathologists and psychiatrists.

The act does not make the amounts paid to health professionals public. Facilities will collect declarations of total working time and pay, but no shared register of these declarations is created.

## What happens next

The act now goes to the Senate, which has 30 days to deal with it, and then to the President, who has 21 days to sign it. The analysis will be updated after each stage, and the changes are recorded in the change history below the text.
