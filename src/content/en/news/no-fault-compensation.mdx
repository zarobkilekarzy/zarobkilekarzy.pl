There is a new text on the site: [No-fault: compensation without blame and a doctor’s liability](/en/mechanisms/no-fault-compensation-and-doctors-liability/). It explains what the word means, how such systems work in Sweden, Denmark, Norway and New Zealand, what is in force in Poland and what the medical self-government wants.

## What it shows

- **No-fault is three different things:** payment to a patient without deciding on fault, limited criminal liability for staff and protection for the person who reports an error.
- **In Denmark and Norway fewer than one in four applicants receives compensation.** Alongside the payments the profession is supervised there by the state, and the criminal route remains.
- **Poland’s Compensation Fund granted 586 payments in 2025.** It does not establish who was at fault and passes the case to no one.
- **The medical self-government calls for removing criminal liability for unintentional acts.** A doctor would then be judged only by medical courts, where 86% of penalties are an admonition, a reprimand or a fine.

## What else is in the text

Data from prosecutors and civil courts on medical error cases, the history of the Polish rules from regional commissions to the Fund, and the arguments for limiting criminal liability. Every number is sourced in the notes.
