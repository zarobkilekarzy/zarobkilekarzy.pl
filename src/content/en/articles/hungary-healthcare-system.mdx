Hungary has a single public payer and state-owned hospitals, and spends a smaller share of national income on health than Poland. In 2021 it reformed the employment of doctors: hospital doctors’ pay was written into an act, additional work was made conditional on consent, and informal payments were made a criminal offence. We describe how this system works according to Hungarian acts and official data, and check what is public in it. We describe the law as it stood on 19 November 2026 — half a year after the change of government.

## One payer, state hospitals

Treatment is paid for by the National Health Insurance Fund Management (NEAK). About 96% of inhabitants are insured.[^profil] The fund is part of the state budget, adopted in the budget act: <Money value={5112604000000} currency="HUF" scale="billion" /> was planned for 2026, 8.6% more than in the plan for 2025.[^budzet]

A person who is not insured on any basis pays the monthly health contribution themselves. If the arrears exceed six times the monthly amount, they lose the right to free services, and paying later does not work retroactively.[^tbj]

Hospitals belong to the state and are subordinate to a central hospital directorate. From 2022 healthcare was overseen by the interior ministry;[^profil] the government appointed in May 2026 created a separate health ministry.[^rzad]

## A cap instead of a price list

A hospital settles with the payer by diagnosis groups, but only up to an annual cap. For services above the cap — up to 10% — it receives 30% of the base rate.[^dekret-43] A family doctor receives a fee for every registered patient, weighted by age; above 2,400 points the rate falls.[^dekret-43]

## What the patient pays

A visit to a doctor and a stay in hospital are free of charge; the patient contributes to the cost of medicines. Even so, out-of-pocket payments by patients account for 22.8% of health spending — clearly more than in Poland (16.6%) and the EU average (15.1%).

The law deals separately with informal payments handed to doctors (*hálapénz*). Since 1 January 2021 the health act has prohibited a healthcare worker from demanding or accepting any benefit other than that provided for by law — during treatment and after it. The exception is a one-off gift in kind after the end of treatment, worth no more than 5% of the monthly minimum wage.[^eutv] The criminal code provides for up to one year’s imprisonment for the person giving and up to three years for the person accepting.[^btk]

Whether informal payments have disappeared is not known. According to the press, by mid-2024 a total of 105 cases had been opened, covering about 250 healthcare workers;[^halapenz-sprawy] we found no conviction statistics.

## Doctors’ work and pay

Since 2021 doctors in state and local-government hospitals have worked under a separate health service employment relationship. Their basic pay is set by a table in an annex to the act and depends only on length of service.[^eszjtv]

The table sets a reference level. After the annual performance appraisal the employer may raise pay by 40% or lower it by no more than 20%; apart from that, pay may not exceed 120% of the amount in the table unless a decree provides otherwise. On-call duty, overtime and supplements are paid separately, at rates set by the central hospital directorate.[^eszjtv] [^dekret-528] For comparison: average gross earnings in the economy in 2025 were <Money value={705000} currency="HUF" /> a month.[^ksh-place]

The table does not cover family doctors or the private sector. Nor did it end the dispute over how much doctors really earn. The ministry stated that the average pay of 10,653 doctors employed full-time was <Money value={2135367} currency="HUF" /> gross in March 2025, with all supplements; the medical chamber calculated about <Money value={1500000} currency="HUF" scale="million" /> from the basic pay alone of the same group.[^lakmusz] Both figures concern about one third of doctors. The statistical office gave an average of about <Money value={1700000} currency="HUF" scale="million" /> gross in December 2024 for doctors employed full-time.[^lakmusz]

## Public and private work at the same time

The Hungarian act requires consent. A doctor in the health service employment relationship may take up additional gainful work only with the prior consent of a designated body — the exceptions include research and teaching. Anyone who takes up work without applying for consent can be dismissed with immediate effect.[^eszjtv] The body has eight days to decide and takes into account the working-time rules and patient safety.[^dekret-528]

Two further restrictions concern the place and the patient. At the seat of their own public hospital a doctor may carry out other healthcare activity only with consent and only in three specified fields, such as dialysis.[^dekret-528] Within the public job the doctor also may not treat a patient whom they previously treated for the same illness outside that job — except in emergencies.[^eszjtv] The provision does not prohibit private practice — it concerns only a patient treated earlier for the same illness.

How many consents were granted and how many refused is not known — we found no such data and no public register of consents.

## What is public

**The place in the queue.** The health insurance act requires waiting lists to be published without personal data, but in such a way that every patient can unambiguously find their place — no later than three days after each change. For this purpose the patient receives their own identifier.[^ebtv] The payer also publishes the number of people waiting and the expected waiting time for each hospital.[^dekret-287]

**The pay table.** A doctor’s basic pay at every step of seniority is written into the act.[^eszjtv]

**Debt — in total.** Every month the State Treasury publishes the sum of the facilities’ liabilities due, without a breakdown by hospital.[^mak]

**The payer’s payments — by type of service.** The payer publishes payments for each budget item, e.g. hospital treatment or primary care, not for individual hospitals.[^neak-wyplaty]

**What is not visible.** The earnings of individual doctors, consents for additional work, or the debt of individual hospitals. The freedom of information act treats as public the name, position and scope of duties of a person performing public tasks, and does not expressly mention pay.[^infotv]

## How many doctors there are and how long people wait

<CountryBars title="Doctors and unmet healthcare needs (Eurostat)" codes={['HU', 'PL', 'EU']} keys={['doctors', 'unmet', 'unmetWait']} />

The waiting-list register covers 15 types of procedure and shows only those cases in which the hospital reports a wait longer than 60 days; it does not cover visits to specialists.[^dekret-287] [^telex-kolejki] According to the payer’s data cited by the press, in September 2026 a total of 21,741 people had been waiting more than 60 days for a knee replacement, 10,607 for a hip replacement and 6,291 for cataract surgery. Patients operated on in the preceding six months had waited an average of 293 days for a knee (median 198) and 143 days for a hip (median 43).[^telex-kolejki]

There were 42,117 working doctors in 2025; the register of doctors lists 76,641 persons. There are 4,166 family doctors for adults — one fifth fewer than in 1998 (5,210) — and each has 2,075 registered patients.[^ksh-lekarze] [^ksh-poz] In July 2026 more than 760 practices were unfilled.[^praktyki]

## Professional and criminal liability

**Professional.** In 2023 parliament abolished compulsory membership of the medical chamber and took ethics proceedings away from it.[^izba-2023] An act in force since 22 October 2026 restores compulsory membership, and from 1 January 2027 ethics proceedings are again conducted by the chamber.[^izba-2026] We found no statistics on such proceedings or on withdrawn licences to practise.

**Criminal.** The criminal code has a separate provision on endangerment in the course of practising a profession. Negligent breach of professional rules that endangered a patient or caused bodily injury carries up to one year’s imprisonment; where the result is serious harm — up to three years, and where it is death — from one to five years.[^btk] We found no statistics on convictions of doctors.

**Civil.** A licence to carry out healthcare activity requires civil liability insurance.[^eutv] We found no system of compensation without establishing fault.

Hungary shows two things at once. First, a public pay table does not answer the question of how much doctors earn: supplements and on-call duty are outside the table, so the ministry and the medical chamber give, from the same data, figures that differ by about HUF 600,000. Second, a waiting list can be public without disclosing names — the patient checks their place by an identifier. The same principle, a permanent identifier instead of a name, is what we propose in Poland for total pay from public money in the demand [“Public amounts, protected names”](/en/demand/).

## Method

We base the description on the consolidated texts of Hungarian acts and decrees, the budget act, data of the statistical office (KSH), the State Treasury and the payer, and the profile of Hungary prepared by the OECD and the European Observatory for the European Commission. The figures on waiting lists, on proceedings concerning informal payments and on doctors’ average pay are known to us from the Hungarian press, which cites official documents — we were unable to open them directly. The comparative indicators for Hungary, Poland and the European Union come from the same Eurostat datasets and the same year.

The amounts in the pay table are basic pay, without on-call duty and supplements. We describe the law as it stood on 19 November 2026.
