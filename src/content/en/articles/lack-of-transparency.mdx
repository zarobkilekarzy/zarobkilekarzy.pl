In a single year a doctor may work in several public facilities: as an employee in one, under a contract in another, on on-call duty in a third. Each pays separately and knows only its own part. Nobody publishes the total amount one person receives from public money.

This text concludes the description of mechanisms on this site. It explains where the amounts we know at all come from, and why for most doctors no such amount exists.

## Each facility sees its own fragment

Payments to one person from different places were not added up even by AOTMiT (the Agency for Health Technology Assessment and Tariff System), the agency that collects pay data in healthcare. Its president admitted in an interview with the daily Rzeczpospolita that the agency sees earnings only at the level of a single facility.[^aotmit] For the agency, the same person in several facilities was several unconnected entries. Since 5 August 2026 this may change, but only on the state’s side — we write about it below.

The gap covers most specialists, because according to AOTMiT data about 73% of them work under civil-law contracts, mainly as contractors.[^aotmit-73] Salaried pay is set by a public act, while nobody collects contract amounts in one place — we describe this difference in [Contract or employment](/en/mechanisms/contract-vs-employment/). What combining facilities looks like in practice is shown by cases from Warsaw, Braniewo, Łosice, Sosnowiec and Płońsk, collected in our text on [holding multiple jobs](/en/mechanisms/multiple-jobs-parallel-contracts/).

## Where the large amounts we know come from

The amounts written about by the media and by this site did not come to light by chance. They arrived by one of three routes, and each route has its limit.

### Asset declarations

Councillors of municipalities, counties and regions, as well as, among others, heads of local-government organisational units, must file asset declarations every year (*oświadczenia majątkowe*). In them they state their income from each source separately. Information from the declarations is public by law, except for the address and location of property, and is published in the Public Information Bulletin (BIP).[^oswiadczenia] For a councillor who is a doctor, the whole picture is therefore visible — including work in several facilities and private practice.

That is why most is known about doctors who are councillors. In the declarations for 2025 that we checked, 14 of 113 doctor-councillors reported at least <Money value={1000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> of income from work, and the highest income was about <Money value={2150000} scale="million" decimals={2} />.[^radni] Rzeczpospolita gave the same highest amount in its own analysis of the declarations.[^radni-rp] We compile the distribution and links to the declarations in our analysis [Doctors who are councillors](/en/analysis/doctor-councillors-public-income/).

Local-government acts do not name hospital directors explicitly, but the declarations of some of them are published. The amounts in two 2026 cases that we describe in our text on [holding multiple jobs](/en/mechanisms/multiple-jobs-parallel-contracts/) come from asset declarations: a doctor who is a councillor in Warsaw, and the director of a county hospital.[^tvn][^losice] In these cases the media publicised amounts that had already been public.

### Media questions and requests for public information

The second route is a question put to a specific facility. The regional hospital in Włocławek gave a local news portal aggregate data for 2025. Of 315 doctors, 245 worked under contracts, 61 as employees and 9 under other civil-law contracts. Payments totalled about <Money value={84000000} scale="million" decimals={0} />, and the highest annual settlement was about <Money value={1470000} scale="million" decimals={2} />.[^wloclawek] This is, however, the amount from one facility. Whether the same person also earned elsewhere does not follow from the answer.

Whether an answer comes at all depends on the facility. The Brzesko Medical Centre treated journalists’ questions about doctors’ pay as “processed information” (*informacja przetworzona*), estimated that the answer would take 32 hours of work, and made its release conditional on payment of <Money value={1500} />.[^brzesk] When Sieć Obywatelska Watchdog Polska, a Polish transparency organisation, sent the same request for payments to doctors to more than a thousand facilities, only a small proportion of them sent full annual payroll lists.[^watchdog] We have compiled these lists in [Disclosed hospital pay lists](/en/data/disclosed-hospital-pay-lists/).

### Inspections

The third route is an inspection. At the Białystok Oncology Centre, a hospital with an NFZ contract worth more than <Money value={400000000} scale="million" decimals={0} />,[^bff] an audit by the regional government office (*urząd marszałkowski*) carried out at the end of 2025 found that one of the doctors had billed almost <Money value={3000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> in 2024. The hospital’s management did not respond to most of the inspection’s findings.[^poranny] At the county hospital in Braniewo, the settlements of a doctor who in 2024 reported more hours than there are in a year were noticed by the new management when it began checking payments.[^braniewo]

An inspection, however, sees only the facility it checks, and acts after the fact. We compile the highest known amounts from all three routes in [Record payments](/en/data/record-doctor-payments/).

## What is nowhere to be found

These three routes share a limit. A declaration is filed only by those whom an act requires to do so. An answer to a question and the result of an inspection concern one facility. For a doctor who is neither a councillor nor a director and works in several facilities, the total amount from public money is not in any public source.

This is not changed by the Central Register of Contracts of Public Finance Sector Units (*Centralny Rejestr Umów*), which receives contracts concluded from 1 July 2026. Units of the sector, including independent public healthcare facilities (SPZOZ), enter into it contracts that are procurement within the meaning of the Public Procurement Law, and the act sets no threshold for their value. The register does not cover contracts for the provision of healthcare services, which the NFZ publishes on its own website. It is public and allows searching for individual contracts, but it does not add them up per person, and information subject to restrictions on access — among other things, for reasons of an individual’s privacy — is not made available in it.[^ufp] A doctor may have several contracts in different facilities, so nobody still sees the total for one person.[^rz-cru]

## The state can calculate, but does not publish

Since 5 August 2026 the minister of health may request from facilities pay data linked to the PESEL number and the licence to practise (PWZ) number. From such data, payments to one person from many facilities can be added up. Facilities, however, provide them only at the minister’s request, and the act does not provide for their publication.[^ustawa-972] We discuss the provisions in our analysis [The pay data act](/en/analysis/pay-data-act-vs-public-register/).

It is different, for example, in Sweden: there, the pay of a person employed by a public authority is a document that anyone may request, without giving a reason.[^st] We describe the comparison in our analysis [Doctors’ pay: Poland compared with its neighbours](/en/analysis/doctor-salaries-poland-vs-neighbours/).

## What follows from this

The missing total works both ways. Settlements that one person could not possibly have worked are not visible. Nor is it possible to show that a doctor who does on-call duty in a neighbouring county, because otherwise the ward would have to close, earns an ordinary amount. Since nobody sees the whole, any amount can be imagined.

That is why we call for the total pay of each person working in facilities with an NFZ contract to be published every quarter as open data: added up across all workplaces, under a permanent identifier instead of a name. We describe the details on the [proposal](/en/demand/) page.
