## The mechanism: an entry is money

The National Health Fund (NFZ) does not see what happens in the consulting room. It sees what the facility reports: the type of service, the date, the patient. Each such record has a price in points — and it is the record, not the visit itself, that turns into a bank transfer.

On top of that there is the plan. A network hospital that in a settlement period reports at least 98% of the points on which its current lump sum was based keeps the lump sum at the full basis in the next period; if it reports less, the basis becomes the number of points actually reported.[^rozp-psz] How this works is described in our text [NFZ payment system](/en/mechanisms/nfz-payment-system/). When points are lacking at the end of the period, the numbers can be “made up” in three ways:

1. admit more patients — that is, do what the system is meant to encourage;
2. report the services performed as more expensive than they really were (so-called upcoding);
3. enter a service that never happened at all.

The third route is the hardest to detect, because the records look correct. The only witness outside the facility–NFZ relationship who knows that the visit did not take place is the patient.

According to NFZ data cited by the health portal Rynek Zdrowia, in 2024 patients lodged almost 3.5 thousand objections to services shown in the Internet Patient Account (*Internetowe Konto Pacjenta*, IKP).[^rynek-zdrowia] More than 1.6 thousand of them turned out to be justified. Most concerned dentistry and rehabilitation — services that are cheap per service, billed in large numbers and rarely checked by patients.

Examples described in the media:[^rzeczpospolita]

- a visit to a rheumatologist at a clinic where the patient had never been treated,
- a denture billed to the NFZ, although the patient never received it,
- rehabilitation reported as reimbursed, even though the patient paid for it privately.

<Aside title="To be fair: not every wrong entry is fraud">

Some irregularities are ordinary mistakes in entering data or errors in a system that is still maturing. That is why a report is verified first. But since more than 1.6 thousand reports turned out to be justified in a single year, the more important question is: how many similar entries has nobody checked?

</Aside>

## Why it has to be the patient who catches it

NFZ inspections are selective: they cover selected facilities and periods, often with a delay. In the first quarter of 2026 the Fund’s inspection departments carried out 490 inspections and verification activities, including 294 inspections under Article 61a of the Act on publicly funded healthcare services.[^dk-2026]

The total financial effect — exactly <Money value={54356609} /> — was made up of refunds of funds paid out unduly (<Money value={23600000} scale="million" />), contractual penalties (almost <Money value={30500000} scale="million" />) and damages (more than <Money value={213000} />).

The largest single penalty in this period — <Money value={1870003} /> — was imposed, as Bankier.pl reported, on a hospital in Rzeszów.[^bankier] According to reports, instead of the procedures actually performed, the facility submitted for settlement much more expensive procedures that had not been carried out.

There are too few inspectors to check every entry. There are enough patients. Anyone who looks at their own visit history becomes part of a distributed audit.

## Check your IKP — instructions

The screenshots show the Polish version of the site, so button and menu names are given in Polish, with a translation.

<Steps title="10 steps · about 5 minutes">

1. Go to **pacjent.gov.pl** and choose “Zaloguj się” (“Log in”) in the top right-hand corner.[^ikp]

   <Screenshot src={ikpLogowanie} alt="Home page of pacjent.gov.pl. In the top right-hand corner, a blue “Zaloguj się” (“Log in”) button." />

2. Confirm your identity: with a trusted profile (*profil zaufany*), in the mObywatel app, with an e-ID card or through your bank. You can set up a trusted profile in online banking.

3. Find the “Historia leczenia” (“Treatment history”) tile and click “Zobacz” (“View”).

   <Screenshot src={ikpHistoriaLeczenia} alt="IKP tiles after logging in. The “Historia leczenia” (“Treatment history”) tile includes, among other things, the visit history; below it, a “Zobacz” (“View”) button." />

4. Go to the section “Świadczenia w ramach Narodowego Funduszu Zdrowia” (“Services under the National Health Fund”) and open “Historia wizyt” (“Visit history”). It lists visits reimbursed by the NFZ since 2008 — together with the cost of reimbursement.[^ikp-pomoc]

   <Screenshot src={ikpSwiadczeniaNfz} alt="The section “Świadczenia w ramach Narodowego Funduszu Zdrowia” (“Services under the National Health Fund”) with the “Historia wizyt” (“Visit history”) tile (visits reimbursed since 2008 and their cost) and a “Zobacz” (“View”) button." />

5. On the “Twoje wizyty” (“Your visits”) list, increase the number of items per page (by default only 5 are shown). Look at the counter of all items and the cost summary. New entries may appear with a delay.

   <Screenshot src={ikpTwojeWizyty} alt="The “Twoje wizyty” (“Your visits”) list: the counter “Wszystkich pozycji 26” (“All items: 26”), outpatient visits with dates and “Szczegóły” (“Details”) buttons, and at the bottom the choice “Pokaż na stronie: 5” (“Show per page: 5”)." />

6. Look for facilities you do not know, dates on which you did not see a doctor, and services you paid for privately that nevertheless appear as reimbursed. Go through dentistry, rehabilitation and medical devices with particular care. The “Szczegóły” (“Details”) button shows exactly what was billed and for how much.

7. Before you report an entry, rule out two false alarms: a teleconsultation is also recorded as a visit, and with subcontracting a different facility name may appear, even though the date matches.

8. If the entry is still unclear, in “Szczegóły” (“Details”) choose “Zgłoś nieprawidłowość” (“Report an irregularity”), fill in your contact details and send it. The report will go to the regional branch of the NFZ.[^nfz-ikp]

9. Do not send the same matter several times — wait for a reply.

10. Also check your child’s account (you have full access until their 18th birthday) and the accounts of relatives, if they have authorised you.
</Steps>

<Button href="https://pacjent.gov.pl">Go to pacjent.gov.pl</Button>

<Aside variant="info" title="When an irregularity is confirmed">

The NFZ removes the service from the settlements, recovers the amount paid for it and deletes the entry from your account, and you receive information about the outcome. The money returns to the system only because someone checked their own history.

</Aside>

<Aside variant="info" title="Other channels and other matters">

- **Objections to NFZ services** can also be lodged via ePUAP (the Polish e-government platform), by post or in person at a regional branch of the NFZ.
- **A wrong prescription or referral** — first contact the facility that issued it; only it can correct or cancel it.
- **Personal data, relatives’ data, health contributions from ZUS/KRUS** (the Social Insurance Institution and the Agricultural Social Insurance Fund) — the e-Health Centre (Centrum e-Zdrowia): helpline 19 239 or the form at [p1pomoc.cez.gov.pl](https://p1pomoc.cez.gov.pl/) (in Polish).

</Aside>
