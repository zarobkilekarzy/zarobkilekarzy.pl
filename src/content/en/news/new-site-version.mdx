Each of the 103 addresses of the old site leads to content — directly or through a redirect; an automated test checks this at every release. The working materials are notes, source data and analyses from which the texts are written — they stay outside the site. We used AI language models in the rebuild; approx. USD 1,100 is the cost of using them.

## What is new

- **Media kit** — [infographics to download](/media/) (in Polish) with the source address and a QR code on each graphic, under the CC BY 4.0 licence.
- **The demand in a new wording** — [“Public amounts, protected names”](/en/demand/): the total pay of each person from all facilities with an NFZ contract, published every quarter under a permanent identifier instead of a name. The previous version spoke of a register by licence number.
- **Draft amendment** — [the text of the draft](/postulat/projekt-ustawy/) (in Polish), which turns the demand into legal provisions.

## What is gone or has moved

- **The petition** is now an archive: [the letters submitted](/postulat/petycja/) as PDFs (in Polish). The old petition addresses lead to the archive.
- **The database of links to press articles** (addresses `/artykuly/`) has not been moved. Its addresses lead to the [list of analyses](/en/analysis/).
- **The health reform monitor** has been removed. Its address leads to the text on the [limit on posts and contracts](/en/mechanisms/multiple-jobs-parallel-contracts/) — the closest topic on the site.
- **The game “Drugi licznik” (“The second meter”)** has not been moved to the new version.
