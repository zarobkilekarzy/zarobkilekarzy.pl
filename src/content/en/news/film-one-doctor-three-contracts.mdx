The site has a new film: “Doba” (“24 hours”), 2 min 37 s with narration in Polish; the English transcript with sources is below the player. It shows one doctor with three contracts: a full-time job paying **PLN 20,000** a month, a contract with a percentage of every procedure and a civil-law contract paying **PLN 30,000** a month. Over a year this comes to **PLN 999,999** — one złoty short of a million. In a single day the three contracts together bill **72 hours**.

The doctor in the film is a model example, and each of his contracts complies with the rules in force. The mechanisms are illustrated by real cases: in Białystok a doctor received almost PLN 3 million for 2024, which the hospital explained by a percentage of procedures, and in Braniewo — according to reports — a doctor billed days of 72 hours. The source of every figure is in the footer of the frame and in the transcript below the film.

## What follows from this

Each workplace sees only its own contract and its own hours. Nobody adds up the total for one person, although the state knows who works where. The act of June 2026 allows the minister to ask for the amounts, but provides neither for their publication nor for a fixed schedule. That is why [our demand](/en/demand/) is an amendment: the total pay of each person from all facilities, every quarter, under a permanent identifier instead of a name. One person, one sum, anonymously.
