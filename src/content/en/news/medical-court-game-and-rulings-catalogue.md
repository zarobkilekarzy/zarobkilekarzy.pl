The site has two new tools on the professional liability of doctors. Both are in Polish and both use the final rulings that the [Supreme Medical Chamber’s portal of rulings](https://nil.org.pl/orzeczenia) (in Polish) has published since 2024.

## Game: would you rule like a medical court?

In the [game](/sad-lekarski/) (in Polish) you read what the court established in a real case and choose a penalty from the scale set by the Act on medical chambers — from acquittal to deprivation of the right to practise. Then you see what the court decided, with a short summary of its reasons.

One round is **10 decisions**. The game picks them so that the share of cases without a penalty, admonitions, reprimands, fines and more severe penalties is close to actual case law, but with some leeway — every round is different.

## Catalogue of rulings

The [catalogue](/sad-lekarski/orzeczenia/) (in Polish) collects **59 decisions from 55 rulings** of regional medical courts and the Supreme Medical Court. Each case has a summary, the charge, the penalty imposed and the name of the file on the chamber’s portal. Cases can be filtered by penalty, and each has its own page that can be shared by link.

## How we describe the cases

We write the descriptions ourselves and keep them more general than the ruling: without places, dates and amounts. We do not give data on the health of the accused or details that would make them identifiable. The catalogue is not a random sample of all rulings and will be expanded.
