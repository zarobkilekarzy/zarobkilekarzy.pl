The site’s texts explain mechanisms. The podcast is a different form: opinion journalism. The speakers argue, and the site says plainly what it thinks. Figures, quotations and descriptions of the rules follow the same standards as in the articles.

## First episode: half-time

The Sejm has passed a requirement of at least a half-time post in hospitals and hospital-based outpatient clinics, and the act is now before the Senate. The site talks to Darek, an anaesthetist on call in a county hospital: what the provision leaves out, where critics are right and what nobody has counted. Darek is a fictional character and uses arguments that were actually made in this dispute.

## How the podcast is made

The sources are public documents, data and statements, listed [with the episode](/podcasty/pol-etatu/#zrodla) (in Polish). AI tools help prepare the script, and the voices are synthetic. Decisions on the claims rest with the editors, who are responsible for the episode.
