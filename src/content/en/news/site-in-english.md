As of 27 October 2026, the site has an [English version](/en/). The language switch is in the header of every page: it leads to the English counterpart or, where there is none, to the English home page.

## What is in English

- [our demand](/en/demand/), "Public amounts, protected names", the about page, common objections and the glossary;
- [patient rights](/en/patient-rights/), with a printable version, a pocket card in PDF and live emergency department queues;
- **39 articles**: 24 analyses, 8 mechanisms and 7 data pages — with the same figures, notes and sources as in Polish.

Some pages stay in Polish only, among them the LEK medical exam quiz, the political barometer, the simulators, the medical court game and "Write to a politician".

## How the translation is made

The texts were translated from Polish with AI and checked against the original by an AI agent. A note on every English page says so and gives the translation date. **The Polish version is authoritative.** When a Polish text changes after translation, the English page gets a notice with both dates and a link to the current original.

Amounts stay in złoty, with an approximate figure in euro at the National Bank of Poland's average rate on the day the site was built.
