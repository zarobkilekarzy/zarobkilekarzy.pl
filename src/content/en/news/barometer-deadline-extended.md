We are extending the deadline for answers to the questions of the [political barometer](/barometr-polityczny/) (in Polish) from 2 to **12 November 2026**. The new deadline applies to all recipients: 11 parliamentary clubs and groups, the KORWiN party and the Supreme Medical Chamber.

We [sent the questions on 18 October](/en/news/barometer-questions-sent/), and to the KORWiN party a day later. As of 30 October we had received no position from any party; one has said it will send its position by 10 November. We are sending a reminder.

## What happens next

- We accept answers until **12 November 2026**. A position may cover only some of the areas; we will mark any area left out plainly.
- On **13 November**, for each recipient that has not answered, we will write “no answer” with the date the questions were sent. On the same day we will publish a summary of who answered and who did not.
- We will publish a late answer as soon as it arrives, with the date we received it.

## Politicians can speak for themselves

Since 26 October, members of the Sejm and Senate and politicians of the parties on the list can make a statement under their own name in the [“Politicians” section](/barometr-polityczny/#politycy) (in Polish). The rules are the same as for parties: the same areas, up to **1,500 characters** in each, no changes to the text. We sign a statement as that person’s view, not as the party’s position.
