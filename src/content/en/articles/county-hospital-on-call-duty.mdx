## Why a doctor has to be on site around the clock

A first-level hospital — the lowest level of the hospital network — must have at least two of the five basic wards (general surgery, internal medicine, obstetrics and gynaecology, neonatology, paediatrics) and a hospital emergency department (SOR) or an admissions room.[^usoz95l] Not every county hospital is a first-level hospital, but these requirements show the minimum that a small hospital has to maintain.

Every ward with patients must have round-the-clock medical care on all days of the week.[^oddzial] The rules allow this care to be combined within wards of the same kind: internal medicine with other non-surgical wards, surgery with other surgical wards. On the maternity ward they require the constant presence of a specialist and of an anaesthetist at the same location, and intensive care may not be combined with anything.

Hospitals make use of this: one doctor is often on duty at night on several wards at once, and sometimes also covers the admissions room. The Supreme Audit Office (NIK) described, for example, a contract for on-call duty in the admissions room and on the neurology ward at the same time.[^nik-zlecanie] Such duty is cheaper for the hospital, but each doctor has more patients.

Nor does every doctor have to stay in the hospital. The act also provides for standby, colloquially called phone duty: the doctor comes in when called, for example when a specialist is needed who is not on site. For an employee, an hour of standby is paid at half the base rate, and from the call onwards the time counts as on-call duty.[^udl98] Where the rules require constant presence — as on the maternity ward — a phone is not enough.

This gives the basic arithmetic: a year has **8,760 hours**, and that is how many have to be staffed at every on-call post, regardless of how many patients there are.

## How much an hour of on-call duty costs

<Comparison table={dutyTable} />

For employees, on-call duty counts as working time and is paid under the Labour Code rules on overtime.[^udl95][^kp1511] From 1 July 2026 the minimum base pay of a specialist is **<Money value={12910.16} />** a month,[^najnizsze] and the opt-out clause makes it possible to work more than 48 hours a week.[^udl96] We show the calculation of an hour at the minimum in our compilation [Distribution of doctors’ pay](/en/data/doctor-pay-distribution/).

One doctor may also combine both forms: be employed and do on-call duty under a contract, in the same hospital or in another one. The rules allow this and leave the choice of form to the parties. NIK noted that higher income, lower contributions and the absence of Labour Code limits lead some staff to swap employment for a contract.[^nik-powiatowe] We describe the consequences of both forms in [Contract or employment](/en/mechanisms/contract-vs-employment/).

No authority collects or publishes contract rates. The notices of competitions in the Public Information Bulletin (BIP) of three county hospitals contain no amounts — the bidder enters the price.[^wodzislaw] The rates that are known come from hospitals’ answers to questions and from statements by their management:

Each row is about something different, so they cannot be combined into an average.[^ilawa][^ostroda][^wadowice][^dzierzoniow][^mlawa] The spread in Mława does, however, show what the market looks like: neighbouring county hospitals bid against each other for the same doctors, while paying from the same NFZ. How this bidding works, including between a public and a private hospital, is described in our text [One doctor, two price lists](/en/analysis/public-and-private-healthcare/#competition-for-doctors).

What this adds up to for a single hospital can be seen where the data have been disclosed. In Iława the average monthly invoice of a contract doctor from January to May 2026 was <Money value={37558.33} />, and 14 doctors invoiced more than <Money value={50000} /> a month.[^ilawa] The hospital in Wadowice paid 89 contract doctors a total of about <Money value={43000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> in 2025. The three highest annual amounts exceeded <Money value={1200000} scale="million" />, and the highest went to an anaesthetist.[^wadowice] Each of these amounts is a payment from one hospital, not the doctor’s whole income.

<Aside title="Why we do not divide the amount by the hours">

Ostróda told a county councillor that in April 2026 one doctor received <Money value={91462.26} /> for 56 hours — which by division gives about <Money value={1633} /> per hour. The hospital explained, however, that the hours cover only the ward and primary care, without the outpatient clinic, procedures and the admissions room.[^ostroda] Without a record of all the hours, such a quotient is not a rate.

</Aside>

## How much a day and a year cost

The cost of one on-call post is the rate times 8,760 hours.

This is an estimate: one post, without cover, the whole day at one rate. A hospital with two wards and an admissions room has several such posts. If it staffed four of them at PLN 200 per hour, on-call duty alone would cost about <Money value={7000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> a year. For comparison: the average county hospital’s revenue from the NFZ in 2025 was about <Money value={131000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> (our calculation from data of the Association of Polish Counties, ZPP: <Money value={27160000000} scale="billion" decimals={2} /> across 207 hospitals), although the spread between small and large hospitals is wide.

For an employee, an hour of on-call duty at the statutory minimum is cheaper, but after on-call duty the doctor must have at least 11 hours of rest, granted immediately after it ends.[^udl97] The hospital therefore needs more doctors than posts — someone to take over the day after the night. This is one of the reasons why hospitals turn to contracts: in the 12 hospitals it audited, NIK found that on average 47% of staff were on civil-law contracts, and that their cost averaged 26% of operating costs.[^nik-zlecanie]

In the financial reports of the 207 county hospitals for 2025, salaries account for 40.73% of costs, and external medical services — where doctors’ contracts are booked, among other things, but also, for example, tests commissioned from outside — for 25.30%.[^zpp26]

## Who pays: from the contribution to the county budget

<NumberedList items={payers} />

The rules of the lump sum are set by the Act on publicly funded healthcare services and a regulation of the minister of health,[^usoz136c][^ryczalt] while covering the loss and taking over debts are governed by the Act on medical activity.[^udl59] The financial data come from reports of the Association of Polish Counties[^zpp26][^zpp27] and from a county council resolution.[^lubliniec] How the lump sum works is described in our text [How the NFZ pays hospitals](/en/mechanisms/nfz-payment-system/).

Instead of covering the loss, some counties lend hospitals money. In April 2026 the Radom County Council lent the hospitals in Iłża and Pionki <Money value={4930000} scale="million" decimals={2} /> for their liabilities for 2025; according to the head of the county (*starosta*), the county had already given the two hospitals more than <Money value={32000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> in total.[^ilza] In Jasło the county lent the hospital <Money value={6000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> interest-free for 10 years.[^jaslo] A loan does not reduce the loss — it shifts repayment to the following years, which the hospital starts with the same lump sum and the same on-call duty.

## When there is no one to take the duty

Money is one side. The other is people: a ward without a full on-call rota cannot admit patients. The hospital in Skarżysko-Kamienna suspended its paediatric ward from 1 April 2025, giving as the reason “a lack of doctors needed to staff the on-call duty” (our translation).[^skarzysko] In Zakopane the obstetrics, gynaecology and neonatal ward is suspended until 31 October 2026, and the hospital is announcing competitions for doctors for regular ward work and on-call duty.[^zakopane] In Lesko a ward where 74 births took place in 2025 was suspended because of a lack of doctors and a renovation, and at the director’s request the NFZ terminated the contract at the end of the year.[^lesko]

## Does every county need a full hospital

Since the cost of on-call duty does not depend on the number of patients, it is highest in the hospitals that have the fewest. In Wadowice the maternity ward was suspended from February 2026: there were 566 births in 2025, against about 1,000 in 2020, and the ward made a loss of <Money value={12000000} scale="million" decimals={0} />.[^wadowice-porod]

One direction of reform is concentration: fewer wards, grouped in larger hospitals. In 2023 NIK named “too many county hospitals” (our translation) as one of the causes of their problems.[^nik-powiatowe] Since 2025 the NFZ may, at a hospital’s request, remove a ward from the network if this serves the consolidation of services in a given area, and in its place conclude a contract, for example for planned treatment.[^usoz159a]

There are also arguments against. A closed ward means a longer journey for the patient, and in childbirth minutes count: at the beginning of October 2026 in Warsaw a woman sent home from hospital gave birth, by her own account, about 20 minutes later. The hospital says that the patient herself wanted to go home.[^miedzylesie] Some county hospitals have recently invested in buildings and equipment. The hospital in Pionki opened new pavilions in 2023 — construction and renovation cost <Money value={86000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> and equipment another <Money value={15000000} scale="million" decimals={0} />, about 40% of it from a government fund. Its surgical ward is to close at the end of 2026,[^pionki] and the county is lending both of its hospitals money for current liabilities.[^ilza]

## The same hours in two places

Nobody adds up the hours that one doctor works in several places. Sometimes the same hours are billed twice. NIK described a hospital that paid two doctors <Money value={16300} /> for hours shown at the same time in two places.[^nik-zlecanie] In the county hospital in Braniewo one doctor billed 11,577 hours in 2024, although a leap year has 8,784 — according to the new management, the same hours were counted under several headings at once. This is an allegation, not an established fact: no one has been charged.[^braniewo][^de-braniewo] We describe this case in more detail in our text on [multiple jobs and parallel contracts](/en/mechanisms/multiple-jobs-parallel-contracts/).

NIK also found on-call shifts lasting 73 hours without a break, and in another audit as many as 124 hours.[^nik-powiatowe][^nik-zlecanie] In that second audit, price was in most cases the only criterion for choosing bids, contracts were concluded even though requirements were not met, because there were not enough doctors for on-call duty, and in 9 of the 12 hospitals a total of <Money value={10300000} scale="million" /> was paid above the maximum value of the contracts.[^nik-zlecanie] The amounts themselves can be seen only when a hospital answers a question. In the monitoring run by Watchdog Poland (Sieć Obywatelska Watchdog Polska), 212 first-level hospitals received a request for doctors’ pay. 76 refused, and 38 sent legible lists. In a typical one, the middle entry is about <Money value={154000} /> a year, and 27 entries exceed <Money value={1000000} scale="million" decimals={0} /> — each of them a payment from one hospital.[^watchdog]

## What the PLN 240 hourly limit will change

The pay limits act, which the Sejm passed on 22 October 2026, provides that the gross hourly rate for services financed by the NFZ may not exceed 1/20 of the minimum wage, i.e. **<Money value={240.3} />** in 2026.[^druk3157][^minimalne] The limit also covers contracts, and the text does not exclude on-call duty from it. It is now before the Senate.[^glosowanie] We follow its stages in [Pay limits for Polish health professionals](/en/analysis/medical-pay-limits-act/).

The president of the National Association of County Hospital Employers (OZPSP) says that “probably nobody will work in an emergency department for PLN 240 an hour”, and at the same time that the limit will become a floor for doctors who earn less today (our translation).[^ozpsp] How many doctors are above this amount and how many below it, nobody has shown — including the explanatory memorandum to the bill.[^druk3157]

## What is not known

It is not known how much county hospitals pay in total for on-call duty, what the distribution of rates is, or how many hours one person works in all hospitals combined. These figures are not in any public register. Until they are, the dispute over whether PLN 240 an hour is too little or too much cannot be settled with data.

Some of these questions would be answered by the total pay of each person from all facilities with an NFZ contract, published every quarter under a permanent identifier instead of a name. Duplicated hours and amounts from several hospitals would then be visible in a single row.
