On 18 October 2026 we sent the questions of the [political barometer](/barometr-polityczny/) (in Polish) to **11 parliamentary clubs and groups** and to the **Supreme Medical Chamber**. Each recipient received a separate message with the same questions.

## What we ask about

The questions cover **6 areas**: funding, staff (doctors, nurses and other health professions separately), pay and transparency, access and waiting lists, hospitals, and the patient. Each area comes with supporting questions — a hint at what is worth covering, not a requirement. An answer in one area may be up to **1,500 characters** including spaces; a party may attach a fuller position as a document.

## What happens next

- We accept answers until **12 November 2026**. The original deadline was 2 November; [we extended it on 30 October](/en/news/barometer-deadline-extended/).
- We publish each answer as submitted, with the documents the party points to. We do not shorten answers or choose documents on a party’s behalf.
- For a party that does not answer, we will say so plainly — “no answer” — with the date the questions were sent.

The barometer page shows the stage of contact with each party.
