There is a new tool for patients on the site: [Find a doctor](/znajdz-lekarza/) (in Polish). It is a skill, a set of instructions for an AI assistant. An assistant that loads it helps to find a specialist doctor in Poland for a specific health problem, in the public system (National Health Fund, NFZ) or privately. The skill and the conversation are in Polish.

## How it works

- **The assistant asks a few questions:** what the problem is, how long it has lasted, where you live, how urgent it is, public or private care.
- **It chooses the specialty and a narrow field**, for example “an orthopaedic surgeon who performs shoulder arthroscopy”, and waits for confirmation.
- **It searches in three places:** a web search engine, the public pages of the ZnanyLekarz portal and the NFZ open data on waiting times.
- **It gives 3–5 doctors.** For each: why the doctor fits, where they see patients, ratings, the price or the waiting time in the public system. Every statement has a link to the page it comes from.

The order is deliberate: **experience with the specific condition first, then availability, patient ratings last**. If the symptoms are alarming, the assistant stops the search and directs the person to the emergency number 112 or to an emergency department.

## Primary care clinics too

The skill also helps to choose a family doctor. It compares clinics by features that can be checked from the outside: coordinated care, online registration, teleconsultations, home visits. Together with the tool, the site has a new guide, [How to change your primary care doctor](/mechanizmy/jak-zmienic-lekarza-poz/) (in Polish): the steps in the Internet Patient Account, the rules, and what happens to the annual rate the NFZ pays the clinic for each patient.

## Limits

The skill does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment and does not book appointments. The list is built from publicly available pages, so it may be incomplete, and an AI assistant can make mistakes. Dates and prices have to be confirmed with the clinic.

The tool is free and its code is open under the MIT licence. Installation takes a few minutes and requires no additional software. The description, the guide and the file to download are on the [Find a doctor](/znajdz-lekarza/) page (in Polish).
